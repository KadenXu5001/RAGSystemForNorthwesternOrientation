Fall 2026 Move-In Information for New First-Year & Transfer Students
Welcome to Northwestern! This website is designed to provide new first year and transfer students with the information you need for the move-in process. If you have questions after reviewing the website, please contact us at (847) 467-HOME or by email at
housing@northwestern.edu.

Returning Student Fall Move In

Fall 2026 Move In Information
Move-In Dates and Times
New first-year and transfer students will move in on Tuesday, September 15, 2026, between 8:00 a.m. and 3:30 p.m.
Most new students will move in on September 15. Scroll to the Early Arrival information below for instructions if you have permission to arrive prior to September 15.
Move-In Process Overview
Prior to Arrival

Shipping and Mailing Items to Campus
Northwestern University Residential Services partners with
University and Student Services (USS)
who provides a Home-to-Room service that allows you to have your belongings waiting for you in your room when you arrive.
Deliveries from USS will take place through September 14.Students moving in prior to September 14 should note that their items might not be delivered until this day.
Acceptance of items for regular mail and package delivery for the residence halls, residential colleges, and special interest communities begins on Tuesday, September 1, 2026. We encourage you to schedule deliveries to arrive after this time.
Mail delivery
to mailboxes will begin on Tuesday, September 15, 2026. For more information about mail and package services, click
here
.
Please note that the campus cannot accommodate storage pod deliveries.
Mini-Fridge Rentals and Bedding/Linens
Northwestern University Residential Services partners with
University and Student Services (USS)
who, in addition to shipping services, provides mini-fridge rentals as well as bedding and linens. Similar to the Home-to-Room service, your items will be placed in your room prior to your arrival if you order by the published deadline.

Information for Early Arrivals
Requests to move on campus as early as 8:00 a.m. on September 9, 2026, are considered based on the needs of the resident, space availability, and appropriate staffing to support students living on campus prior to the designated move-in dates. If approved to move on campus early, residents may be placed in a temporary housing assignment. The charge for early arrival is $50 per night and is applied to the student account unless the stay is sponsored by a school or department. Any student who desires early arrival housing should review the
Undergraduate Early Arrival Request
page
for additional information. The early arrival application will be available on the
Housing Portal from August 1-21, 2026
. Students who arrive early but are not approved will be charged $100 per night. See the
early arrival page
for full details.
International Wildcat Welcome
occurs before the general new student move-in date. See the next section on this page for additional information regarding international student arrival.
Students who are approved for early arrival do not need an unloading permit and should be aware that parking policies are enforced between 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. Monday-Friday. Parking is generally free in any non-reserved space on the weekends and between 4:00 p.m. and 8:00 a.m. on weekdays.
Please note that move in assistance is only available on September 15 due to the need to move a high volume of students over a short period of time. During all of move-in, carts are available at area desks.
If you have a Wildcard, on the day of your scheduled early move in:
Check in using the Self-Check In link that will be sent to your Northwestern email address
Go to your building and use your Wildcard to enter the building and go to your room
You can also go to your
area desk
to check in and borrow a moving cart
At your room, tap your Wildcard once on the card reader, wait 60 seconds for your access credentials to download, then tap again for entry
If you have any trouble, contact or visit your
area desk
If you do not have a Wildcard, on the day of your scheduled move in:
Check in using the Self-Check In link that will be sent to your Northwestern email address and posted in your building lobby
Go to your
area desk
when you arrive on campus to obtain a Wildcard or temporary access card
Go to your building and use your Wildcard or temporary access card to enter your building and go to your room
At your room, tap your Wildcard or access card once on the card reader, wait 60 seconds for your access credentials to download, then tap again for entry
If you have any trouble, contact or visit your
area desk
International Student Arrival
New international students move in on Sunday, September 13, 2026, at no additional cost. Students arriving before this day are responsible for the early arrival housing charges based on the date they move in and must complete an
early arrival request form
to notify us of when you plan to arrive to campus.  The cost is $50 a night for students who are approved to arrive prior to September 13. Each student will be responsible for these additional charges that will be applied to your student account.
Review the move-in instructions in the section above labeled "Early Arrivals." Please note that all international students arriving early will need to check in at their
Area Desks
to receive temporary access cards.
International students with questions about arriving prior to September 9, 2026, should email us at
housing@northwestern.edu
.
Move In Day Activities
After moving in, students and family members will participate in different tracks of activities. Events for students include:
4:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. - Residential College welcome events
5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. - Community meetings and dinner with Resident Assistants
8:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. - First Night Northwestern events
